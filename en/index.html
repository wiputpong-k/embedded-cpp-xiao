    How to study

    Take one lesson a day, in order: 25 lessons, about 12 hours in total. Each lesson goes goal → concept + diagram → hands-on → check questions, and builds on the one before. The code from lessons 07, 10 and 13–14 is tested and built in CI near the end.

    Lessons 00–14 use Arduino IDE 2 with the Seeed nRF52 (non-mbed) core, the fastest way to see results. The language rules are the same in every framework, so you can move to nRF Connect SDK / Zephyr right after.

    Your progress and last opened lesson are remembered in this browser, shared between Thai and English.
